A privacy-first public-interest project to help families find safe next steps, preserve original information, and route serious concerns to qualified people who can act.

If someone is in immediate danger

Call your local emergency number. In the United States, call 911. Do not wait for this website or a digital tool to respond.

01 · Safety first

Find human help

In the United States, the Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline offers support at 1-800-422-4453. If your device may be monitored, use a safer device if possible.

Visit Childhelp's official website

02 · Preserve, don't alter

Keep originals safe

Keep original files unchanged, note when and how they were obtained, and restrict access. Do not confront a suspected person or publish a child's identity.

03 · Facts before claims

Separate evidence from conclusions

Record what you personally observed, what someone told you, and what remains unverified. Hashes can reveal later file changes; they do not prove an allegation is true.

04 · Privacy by design

No report intake on this page

This prototype has no submission form, file upload, analytics, or AI processing. Do not send us a child's name, images, location, or case details through public repositories or issue trackers.

Private local tool · Prototype

Guardian Evidence Vault

The companion command-line tool encrypts a file locally before storing it, keeps original filenames and file hashes inside the encrypted envelope, and verifies the content when decrypted. It does not upload data.

It is an early prototype, not independently audited, and not suitable as the only copy of evidence. Keep a protected backup of the key separately from the encrypted vault. Never use a shared or compromised device for sensitive material.

Reporting and support

Choose an appropriate local child-protection agency, law-enforcement agency, licensed attorney, or trained victim advocate based on the situation and your location. Requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Accountability without exposure

Preserve records and use lawful reporting channels. Do not dox, threaten, harass, or publicly accuse people based on unverified information. Protecting children includes protecting their identities and due process.
